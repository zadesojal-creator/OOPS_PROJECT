# ClinicQueue Multispecialty Clinic & Healthcare Center
## Comprehensive Hospital Information & Patient Guide

### 1. Hospital Overview & Mission
ClinicQueue Multispecialty Clinic is a modern, state-of-the-art outpatient healthcare facility dedicated to providing compassionate, efficient, and affordable medical care. Our facility integrates smart walk-in digital queues, pre-scheduled specialist appointments, an in-house digital pharmacy, and advanced pathology diagnostics under one roof.

### 5. Medical Departments & Specialties
ClinicQueue offers specialized outpatient care across 8 primary departments:
1. **General Medicine & Family Practice**: Comprehensive diagnosis, acute illnesses, diabetes, hypertension, fever, and routine checkups.
2. **Cardiology**: Heart health, ECG, 2D-Echocardiogram, blood pressure management, and cardiovascular risk screening.
3. **Dermatology & Cosmetology**: Skin, hair, and nail conditions, allergy testing, acne, eczema, and laser dermatology.
4. **Neurology**: Headaches, migraines, epilepsy, nerve disorders, neuropathy, stroke rehabilitation, and memory care.
5. **Orthopedics & Joint Care**: Bone fractures, joint pain, arthritis management, spine care, and sports injury recovery.
6. **Pediatrics & Child Health**: Newborn care, child growth assessment, immunizations, childhood infections, and pediatric wellness.
7. **ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat)**: Hearing assessments, sinus treatment, throat infections, tonsil evaluations, and vertigo care.
8. **Dental & Oral Health**: Teeth cleaning, digital X-rays, root canals, braces, fillings, and pediatric dental hygiene.

- **Health Insurance & Cashless TPA**: We are empanelled with all major health insurance providers. Patients with valid health insurance cards can avail cashless outpatient coverage or get instant claim reimbursement paperwork.

### 8. Smart Queue Management & Priorities
ClinicQueue operates a fair, modern triage queue system:
- **Emergency Priority**: Immediate priority consultation without waiting for acute trauma, severe chest pain, shortness of breath, or critical symptoms.
- **Senior Citizen Priority**: Patients aged 60 and above automatically receive priority queue status over standard tokens.
- **Normal Walk-in Priority**: First-In, First-Out (FIFO) token allocation.
- **Digital Tracking**: Patients can check their live line position (`#X in queue`) anytime via our website or Telegram bot.

### 9. Laboratory & Diagnostic Facilities
- Complete Blood Count (CBC), Lipid Profile, Liver & Kidney Function Tests (LFT/KFT), HbA1c, Thyroid Panel (TSH, T3, T4).
- 12-lead Digital ECG, Holter monitoring, 2D Echocardiography.
- Digital X-Ray (low radiation) and High-Resolution Ultrasound / Sonography.
- Fast turnaround: Routine blood tests available in under 2 hours; digital reports sent via SMS & WhatsApp.
